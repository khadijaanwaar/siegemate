Sit at a table. Hands or Touch controllers both work.

1. Open https://siegemate.pages.dev in the Meta Quest browser and press Play on your table. The first visit needs the internet; after that it plays offline.
2. Look at the table until a ring sits on it, then pinch (or pull the trigger) to place the board. No table? After 8 seconds it stands at seat height.
3. You play white against the computer at Knight strength. Pinch a soldier and let go on a glowing square. The computer answers.
4. Take a piece to see a fight. Pinch both edges of the board to move or resize it.
5. Open Menu (on the slab beside the board, or A/X). Change Battleground to Frostfall or Siege at Dusk and watch the room change. Height up/down, Sound and Music are there too.
6. At the end, Share this battle on the result card shows a QR code of the replay link.

Exit asks twice. Holding the Meta button to recentre keeps the board on the table.
