What excites me is mixed reality that uses the room you are already sitting in. With passthrough and hand tracking, a kitchen table can hold a battle of living toy soldiers that you command with your own fingers, and the room itself can join in: the lights going down, snow or embers in the air. That is something a flat screen cannot do.

I am also excited that a headset game can be a link. In the Quest browser there is nothing to install, it works offline after the first visit, and a finished game can be passed to a friend as a single address or a QR code.

Next I want two people to sit at their own tables, in different places, and play on the same board, each seeing the other's hands as a faint ghost across it.
