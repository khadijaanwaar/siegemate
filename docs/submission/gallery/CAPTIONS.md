# Devpost gallery: shots and captions

Eight images for the Devpost gallery, in upload order. Every file is **3:2, 2160×1440, JPEG quality 88**, sRGB.
All are emulator captures (Playwright + IWER, scripted hands, the photographed room behind passthrough) and carry the
corner label **"Emulator capture · simulated room"** (bottom left, Inter 600, 30 px, cream at 70% on a dark pill).
Number 08 is a 2D composite and carries no label.

None of these repeats a README framing (`docs/images/readme/SHOTLIST.md`): different moments, angles and battlegrounds.
The caption is the text to paste under each image on Devpost.

Common setup unless a shot says otherwise: board on the simulated room's wooden table, default size (1.15×), Knight
strength, coach hints already completed, no `?debug` card.

---

### 01-the-board-lands.jpg

**Shot:** The moment of placement. Seated eye at 1.20 m, looking down about 35° at the table. The placement ring sits
on the tabletop and the board has just appeared inside it, Ivory Kingdom (near) against Sun Empire (far), every figure
in its idle pose. Coach card "Pinch to place" fading out above the board. Battleground **Dawn Court** (room untouched).
The right hand at the bottom right of frame, fingers just opening from the pinch.

**Caption:** Pinch, and the board stands on your real table. No table in sight? After eight seconds it waits at seat height instead.

### 02-command-from-across-the-table.jpg

**Shot:** Touch controller play. Camera behind and above the player's right shoulder (eye 1.35 m), the right Touch
controller in frame, its ray running to the board. A Sun Empire knight figure (black, the player's side; the board is
turned so black is near) is ordered along the ray: legal squares lit, the hover ring on the target square. Battleground
**Dune Bastion** (warm light veil, a thin drift of sand in the far background, none near the face).

**Caption:** Playing black with Touch controllers: trigger a figure, drag the ray to a lit square. Every move works with bare hands too.

### 03-a-volley-of-fire.jpg

**Shot:** A caster's capture, seen from the far side of the board at figure height, about 25 cm out, so the player's
side faces camera. An Ivory Kingdom sorceress (queen) throws her volley of three bolts down the file at a Sun Empire
figure; the bolts lighting the board, the target starting to burn away. Battleground **Sun Temple** (light green veil,
pollen motes). No panels in frame.

**Caption:** Every capture is a fight. The sorceress throws three bolts and leaves the square burning.

### 04-crown-the-pawn.jpg

**Shot:** Promotion. The player's view, eye 1.20 m. A white pawn stands on the last rank waiting, and the promotion card
stands on the player's edge of the board: "Promote your pawn to", Queen, Rook, Bishop, Knight. The right index
fingertip hovering on Queen (hover state lit). Battleground **Stormwatch** (cool dark veil, rain streaks away to the
sides, none in front of the face).

**Caption:** Reach the last rank and a card appears on your side of the board. Poke Queen, Rook, Bishop or Knight.

### 05-frostfall-in-the-room.jpg

**Shot:** Wide, from the player's left at seated height, 1.5 m from the table, so the board is the right third of the
frame and the room fills the rest. Battleground **Frostfall**: cool blue veil, snow falling through the room around the
player and parting over the board, a cool light pool on the tabletop. Mid-game position, Grande Armée against Ivory
Kingdom. The player's head and shoulders just out of frame.

**Caption:** Pick Frostfall and it snows in your room. The weather keeps at least 70 cm from your eyes and parts around the board.

### 06-make-it-your-size.jpg

**Shot:** Two-hand resize. Player's view, both hands in frame pinching the left and right edges of the board, pulled
apart; the board mid-grow (about 1.4×). Battleground **Dawn Court**. Control slab visible to the right.

**Caption:** Pinch both edges to move, turn and resize the board from 0.6× to 1.6×. Height and size are remembered.

### 07-share-the-battle.jpg

**Shot:** The end of a game. The headset result card "VICTORY" with the record line, Rematch, Exit and "Share this
battle", square on from about 45 cm, over the final position. Battleground **Siege at Dusk** (warm veil, embers).
(Pressing Share puts the QR card in the result card's place; the README's `share.jpg` shows that side.)

**Caption:** Win, and the result card offers Share this battle: it turns the whole game into a QR code of a replay link. No server, no account.

### 08-the-replay-anywhere.jpg

**Shot:** A 2D composite on a plain dark background (#15171C): on the left, the 1200×630 battle card
(`docs/share/battle-card.png`) at about 60% of the width; on the right, a phone-width capture of the replay page
(`docs/share/replay-phone.png`) playing the same game. Small Inter caption under each: "Battle card" and "Replay on a
phone". No emulator label.

**Caption:** On a phone or computer, Share sends a battle card and the link. Opening it plays the game back with the same armies, even offline.
